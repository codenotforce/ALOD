\begin{abstract}
We develop an a posteriori certification framework for a Petrov--Galerkin
localized orthogonal decomposition method for the Helmholtz equation.  In
contrast to reference-solution estimates, the proposed estimator controls
the error with respect to the exact variational solution.  The construction
combines a compatibility-corrected equilibrated-flux majorant for the full
residual with computable primal and adjoint defects measuring the distance
between the localized discrete correctors and their continuous ideal
counterparts.  A finite-dimensional stability transfer converts the
computed Petrov--Galerkin inf--sup constant into a certified lower bound for
the continuous ideal LOD pairing.  Under a directly checkable defect
condition, the reliability constant is independent of the wavenumber, the
coarse and fine mesh sizes, and the oversampling depth.  The resulting
adaptive method uses inexpensive local Riesz indicators for coarse marking
and evaluates the more costly exact-solution certificate only at selected
checkpoints.  Fine-scale matrices are retained over cache batches and are
rebuilt only on affected patches.  Two manufactured benchmarks, a localized
highly oscillatory solution and a low-regularity L-shaped problem, are
specified to assess reliability, effectivity, refinement patterns, and
computational cost.
\end{abstract}
